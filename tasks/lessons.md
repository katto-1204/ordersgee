# Project lessons

- When Google Sheets is described as the database, implement record persistence and retrieval with stable IDs, deletion tracking and offline local saves. A CSV export or reporting-only sheet does not satisfy that requirement.
- Keep a team's photos and designs attached to its specific order. Include finished and archived orders in the team register so photos can be collected afterwards.
- Visual QA must include the dashboard's first-run state and the reopened Guide dialog. Passing behavior tests and inspecting other pages cannot prove that every visible component has its styles.
